Privacy Policy

GOBBLER POS Backup — last updated 21 October 2026. Back to the home page.

Who this applies to

GOBBLER POS Backup is a private, single-user tool. Its only user is its owner, who connects it to their own Google account. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from anyone else.

Google user data the app accesses

The app requests one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it the app can create, read, change and delete only the files that the app itself created in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other file, and it does not read email, contacts, calendar, photos or any other Google data.

The files the app creates are encrypted backup files and small text records (checksums and run logs) stored in one folder.

How the data is used and stored

  • Use: solely to store, verify, list, download and delete the owner's own encrypted backups. Nothing is used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.
  • Encryption: backups are encrypted before upload. Google and anyone else who sees the files in Google Drive cannot read their contents without the owner's passphrase, which is not stored in Google Drive.
  • Credentials: the Google authorisation token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository.
  • Processing: backups are created on GitHub-hosted runners that run the owner's workflow, and are not kept there after the run ends.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. No third party receives it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

GOBBLER POS Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Backups are kept according to a retention rule set by the owner and older ones are deleted by the app. The owner can stop the app at any time by removing its access in their Google Account (Security, third-party access) and can delete the backup folder from Google Drive. Removing access prevents the app from reading or writing any Google data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone other than its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
